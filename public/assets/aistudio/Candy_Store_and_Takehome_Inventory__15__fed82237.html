Candy Store and Takehome Inventory

Users sign in with their username and password; passwords are stored as a salted hash and are never shown, so leave a Password box empty to keep it. Access: tick All to let a user change everything; otherwise tick what they may do: View Only (look at data but not edit it), and Inventory Location, Start Date or Cut Off Date (may change that setting). Restrictions block one of those changes even for a user with All access. Only users with All Location, All access and no restrictions (administrators) can manage User Access, the Locations list and Cashier Names, and only they can delete data (one line or all) and change history. This sign-in protects the page in this browser only; it is not a substitute for a secured server.

All amounts are peso values: each quantity encoded in Data Entry multiplied by its SRP, added up for the week. Beginning Inventory = the previous week's Actual Ending Inventory (plus the opening quantity of any item first entered that week). Total Available = Beginning Inventory + Deliveries + Stock Withdrawal + Transfer FROM. Total to be deducted = Transfer TO + Bad Order. Total Net Available = Total Available − Total to be deducted. Actual Ending Inventory = the week's ending inventory. Should be - Sales is the Grand Total Sales of that Weekly Summary, Cash on Hand is the Cash on Hand of that Weekly Summary, and Variances (over/short) = Cash on Hand − Should be - Sales. Cashier Payment is typed in; Balance = Variances + Cashier Payment. The Summary column covers the whole period: Beginning from Week 1, Actual Ending from the last week, and the other rows added up.

Import items

Choose an Excel file (.xlsx) with the header row, or paste rows copied from Excel. Lines whose Line No. already exists are updated; the others are added. Use Export to get an Excel file in the right layout.

Changes save automatically in this browser. A price change gets its own line (same item code, new SRP). Data Entry pulls the SRP from the line you pick. There is one Item Master for every location: all items appear in the Data Entry search at every Inventory Location. Unit Cost is calculated automatically: Delivery Cost ÷ Conversion.

Pick a Date to see only the entries for that date. Shift beside the Date switches between the AM Shift and PM Shift entries of that date. Each shift keeps its own rows, totals and Cash Details, and a PM Shift row's Beginning Inventory comes from the AM Shift's Ending Inventory. Export (Download) adds a Shift column and Import (Upload) reads it (rows without one are treated as AM Shift). Picking a date automatically lists every item whose previous Ending Inventory is more than zero, with that quantity as its Beginning Inventory. Carried-over rows stay saved; use × to remove an item you don't want listed. Dates outside the Start and Cut Off dates don't carry items forward. Items are grouped by category (A to Z) with a Total after each category and a Grand Total at the bottom. Search the Item Description by name, code, category or SRP. Month Week Number counts 7-day weeks from the Start Date. Total Goods Available = Beginning + Deliveries + Transfer from + Internal Transfer From − Transfer To − Internal Transfer To. Total Goods Sold = Total Goods Available − Ending Inventory, so a blank or 0 Ending Inventory means the item sold out: Total Goods Sold equals Total Goods Available, and the box shows "Sold out". Total Sales = Total Goods Sold × SRP. Price-change totals turn red if out and in don't match. The column headings stay frozen while you scroll. Add row starts a blank row on the picked date. Cash Details sit below the table: Should be Sales is the date's total sales (the sum of the category totals), Cash on Hand is typed in, and Over/Short = Cash on Hand − Should be Sales. Import (Upload) adds entries from an Excel file in the same columns as Export (Download), matching each Item Description (and SRP) to the Item Master; Total rows in the file are ignored.

The column headings stay frozen at the top while you scroll. Each week tab groups Item Master lines by category, with a Total row per category. Beginning comes from a line's first entry in the week, Ending from its last entry, and the other quantities are added up. The Monthly Summary covers the whole period from the Start Date to the Cut Off Date: Beginning comes from the first day and Ending from the cut off date. Cash Details: Should be Sales is the sum of the category Total Sales shown above, Cash on Hand adds up the daily cash typed in Data Entry for those dates, and Over/Short = Cash on Hand − Should be Sales.
